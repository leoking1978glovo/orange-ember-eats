# Punto Verde

Build a bold, maximalist restaurant landing page with an energetic, confident vibe. Think of a modern urban eatery with strong personality — like a trendy burger joint, taco spot, or fusion bistro.

Design requirements:

- Color palette: vibrant orange (#FF6B35) and warm yellow (#F7C137) as primary accents, with deep black (#0A0A0A) and off-white (#FAFAFA) as neutrals.

- Typography: very large, bold headlines (up to 72px on desktop), tight letter-spacing on headers, clean sans-serif body text.

- Layout: full-width sections, generous whitespace, asymmetric grids where appropriate.

- Navigation: transparent navbar that becomes solid black on scroll, with a centered logo and minimal links: Menu, Reservations, About, Contact.

- Hero section: full-screen height, large animated headline text (e.g. "Taste the Revolution"), a subtle gradient or texture background, and a prominent "Book a Table" CTA button.

- Featured dishes: a horizontal scrollable carousel of signature dish cards showing high-quality food photo, dish name, short description, and price. On hover, show a "View Details" overlay.

- Menu preview: a grid section showing categories (Starters, Mains, Desserts, Drinks) with 3-4 items per category, each with name, description, and price.

- About / Story section: full-width with a large image on one side and text on the other, telling the restaurant's story.

- Reservation CTA: a bold section with a booking form (name, email, date, time, party size) and a large orange submit button.

- Video section: a full-width autoplay video section (use a placeholder video URL) showing kitchen atmosphere or food preparation, with overlay text.

- Footer: dark background, address, opening hours, phone, newsletter signup, social links.

Tech stack: React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion for animations.
